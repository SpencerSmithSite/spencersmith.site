A citation you can check

Ask a general-purpose chatbot what Athanasius said about the incarnation and it will tell you, fluently and sometimes from nothing. Council answers only out of texts it actually holds, names them, and lets you open the passage and read around it.

Primary texts, not summaries

Every work is the real thing: the acts of the seven ecumenical councils, not a paragraph about them. Each source records its translator, edition and where it was digitised from.

Nothing leaves unless you say so

The library, the search index and the embedding model all live on your device. Council ships with no AI backend selected, so it is a search tool until you choose otherwise.

Two kinds of search at once

Exact terms like homoousion, a quoted phrase or a proper name are found by full-text search, and meaning by on-device embeddings. The two rankings are fused, so neither blind spot survives.

21 traditions, side by side

Reformed, Baptist, Anabaptist, Quaker, Lutheran, Anglican, Methodist, Catholic, Eastern and Oriental Orthodox, the early church, the ecumenical councils and more, so a question about baptism can be answered by all of them at once.

Read, highlight, annotate

Tap any passage to highlight it in one of several colours, write a note against it, bookmark it, or ask a question about that passage without losing your place.

Download only what you want

The King James Bible is built in. Everything else arrives as a collection you choose, from 8.9 MB of creeds and confessions up to the complete fathers.

Start small, or take all of it

Council installs with the complete King James Version, all 1,189 chapters, and is useful before a single download. Collections overlap freely, and no work is ever downloaded twice however many collections it belongs to.

Three ways to answer a question

Council does not ship with a model, does not proxy your questions through anyone's server, and has no account to create.

  1. Search only Default

    No AI at all. Council retrieves the passages and shows them to you. Nothing is generated and nothing leaves the device.

  2. Ollama Local

    A model on your own machine, or another you can reach over your network or VPN. Answers are generated locally and stay there. How to set it up

  3. Your own API key Leaves device

    Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok, billed to your own account and stored in the system keychain. Council states plainly that this is the one option that sends your question off the device. Getting a key

Free, and free of accounts

No sign-up, no subscription, no telemetry, no ads.
