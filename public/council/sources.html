    The library only holds works that are public domain in the United States and available as real, checkable text. If something you need meets that bar and is not here, say so — the corpus grows by request more than by plan.

    Deliberately absent, and not faked: Pentecostal standards, and the confessional documents of the Oriental Orthodox churches — both 20th-century and still in copyright. The Tewahedo scriptural canon is here; their modern standards are not. A gap is better than an invention.
